Privacy Policy — Following Manager for X

Last updated: [ADD DATE BEFORE PUBLISHING]

What this extension does

Following Manager for X is a browser extension that helps you review and manage your own X (Twitter) following list from within your existing, logged-in browser session.

What data we access

When you click "Scan," the extension reads your own following list (handles, display names, bios, verification status, mutual-follow status, follower counts, and last-tweet date) directly from x.com using your existing logged-in session. It does not access your password, DMs, emails, or any account other than the one you are logged into.

Where your data is stored

All scanned data, settings, and unfollow history are stored locally on your device using the browser's built-in storage (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is transmitted to any external server, and we do not operate any backend that collects or receives this data. Uninstalling the extension permanently deletes all locally stored data.

What actions the extension performs

The extension only unfollows accounts you explicitly select and confirm. It does so by calling the same requests X's own web client uses, or by clicking the visible Unfollow button, using your own session — no separate credentials are used or stored.

Third parties

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party. The extension does not include analytics, ad, or tracking code.
